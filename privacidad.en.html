Privacy Policy

At HotelBookings, we respect the privacy of people who visit our website. This Privacy Policy explains how our website operates, what information may be involved when you use its services, and who is responsible for personal data provided through external services.

HotelBookings is an informational website that allows users to explore information about destinations and accommodation and access external search and booking services, including Expedia's search engine. Bookings and services provided by third parties are governed by those providers' terms and policies.

2. Individual bookings through Expedia

When a person uses the Expedia search engine, links or booking tools available on HotelBookings, they may be redirected to a website or service operated by Expedia or entities related to that platform.

The personal data required to make and manage individual bookings is collected by Expedia in accordance with its own Privacy Policy and the terms applicable to the service.

HotelBookings does not control Expedia's systems or the data processing practices applied by that platform. The processing of data entered into its external services is subject to its privacy policies and terms. We recommend reading them before providing personal data or completing a booking.

3. Group booking requests

HotelBookings may offer a form or contact channel for group accommodation requests. For group bookings, the personal data provided in the request is collected directly by the accommodation provider receiving the booking request, which will be responsible for processing that data in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable law.

HotelBookings does not control the privacy practices, systems or data processing decisions of the receiving accommodation provider once it has received the request. Questions regarding the subsequent use of that data should be directed to the relevant accommodation provider.

4. Information HotelBookings may receive

HotelBookings seeks to limit the information it receives to what is necessary to operate its website and respond to communications sent to it. Depending on how the website is used, it may receive information that users include in a message addressed to HotelBookings, such as their name, email address and the content of their enquiry.

If HotelBookings were to collect personal data directly, its processing would be governed by the purposes, retention conditions and other criteria described in Section 7 of this Privacy Policy, as well as by any applicable legal obligations.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

HotelBookings does not install or use its own tracking cookies to track visitor activity, create advertising profiles or monitor behaviour on the website.

However, some links or tools may direct users to third-party services, such as Expedia. These external services may use their own cookies or other technologies in accordance with their privacy practices and policies. HotelBookings does not control the cookies that such third parties may install when users access their platforms.

For information about cookies and technologies used by an external provider, please consult that provider's published privacy and cookie policies.

6. Links to third-party websites

This website may include links to pages, search engines, accommodation providers and other services operated by third parties. These links are provided to make it easier for visitors to access information or services that may be useful.

HotelBookings takes reasonable measures to protect information it receives directly against unauthorised access, use, alteration or disclosure. HotelBookings will never share data that has been provided to it directly.

7. Purpose and retention of information

When HotelBookings receives information directly from a person, it may use it to respond to an enquiry, manage a communication, handle a request related to its services or maintain the security and operation of the website.

HotelBookings will retain information received directly for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfil these purposes, address potential issues and comply with applicable legal obligations. When the information is no longer necessary, it will be deleted or no longer used, as appropriate and to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The retention periods applicable to data collected directly by Expedia or other providers are governed by those providers' policies, not by HotelBookings' retention practices.

8. Information security

HotelBookings takes reasonable measures to protect information it receives directly against unauthorised access, use, alteration or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

HotelBookings does not administer the internal systems of external providers and cannot guarantee the security of information provided directly to those providers. For questions concerning data sent to Expedia or an accommodation provider, users should contact the provider that received the data.

9. Rights of data subjects

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, individuals may have the right to request access to their personal data, its rectification or erasure, restriction of processing, or to object to certain uses, as well as to exercise other rights recognised by the relevant legislation.

If the information was provided directly to HotelBookings, you may send your request to the contact email address listed on this page. To exercise rights regarding data collected or processed by Expedia, an accommodation provider or another external provider, you must contact that provider directly, as it is responsible for handling requests concerning data under its control.

HotelBookings will assess requests it receives in relation to the information it processes and respond in accordance with its applicable obligations.
